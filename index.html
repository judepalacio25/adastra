Lesson 1: Efficiently design a website layout for easy navigation
Have you ever visited a website and you get immediately confused because you can’t find what you’re looking for due to its layout? May it be that you're a kid or an adult, designing your layout to be easy for navigation is ideal and useful for all users. Having a clear layout will help your users find the content they need from your website. In this lesson, you will learn how to efficiently design a website layout for easy navigation.
Lesson objectives:
-Identify key elements of a website layout.
-Explain why clear navigation improves user experience.
-Design a simple webpage layout that allows users to find information easily.
What makes a website easy or difficult to use? As we all know, having an easy or user friendly experience, for these are one of the factors that ensures the audience of your website comes around every now and then to check put your website. Everyone wants to have a good experience while visiting a website. No wants a website that is confusing, challenging or difficult to use. When creating a website always know that, better layout always equal a better user friendly experience.
Lesson 2: Make sure that your textual content is clear, relevant and memorable. Also how to effectively communicate your website's intended message.
We can’t deny that the visuals of a website is important since it brings engagement and attracts users but we can’t also deny that the textual content of it is also important. It should be clearly written and does not confuse the readers, it should also be relevant depending on the website’s topic and most of all, it should be memorable to ensure that users revisit the website. It should also be taken into consideration with how the textual content is communicated. In this lesson you’ll learn how to make your textual content clear, relevant and memorable as well as how to effectively communicate it.
Lesson objectives:
-Students will understand how to create clear, relevant, and memorable textual content and learn how to effectively communicate a website's intended message.
On one hand, good website would always have a clear goal or initiative, it would always have a proper message and direction. On the other hand, a bad website doesn't have a clear motive nor reason for existing. It doesn't have a proper message meaning there is no reason for you to visit it. Your content must always be clear and your message must always be related to the theme of your website. If one thing about your website is confusing, or disconnected all of it will stop making sense, or it would just be borderline confusing. That is why you must have a clear message that is related to the intent of your website.
Lesson 3: Provide clear and user-friendly functionality.
Back in the good old days, websites were known to be static, this means that the website wasn’t that much engaging and you can’t interact much with it. Nowadays, websites being interactive are highly needed, in line with this, it also needs to be user- friendly to avoid user confusion. In this lesson, you’ll learn how to provide a clear and user-friendly functionality for your own website.
Lesson objectives:
-Students will understand how to design websites with clear and user-friendly functionality so users can easily navigate and interact with the site.
Simplicity is key. To minimize confusion or eliminate it all together, don't be afraid to make your website layout as simple as possible. This won't make you website boring or lame, it will only ensure that your website is clear, easy to follow, and understandable.
Lesson 4: Make use of interactive elements for your website.
A common problem with some websites is that it’s not engaging enough making it forgettable and unattractive. However it should be noted that interactive elements must be balanced along with the layout and other elements. In this lesson, you will learn what interactive elements can be used and how to effectively use it.
Lesson objectives:
-Students will learn how interactive elements can make a website more engaging and improve the user experience.
While clarity is a must, you must also find ways to hook your audience. Interactive elements in your website ensure this. Interactive elements are fun ways to keep your audience entertained, whether there just scrolling through your website or thinking, the interactive elements our ways to ensure that they won't be bored and this could be a factor that will make them remember your website, and it could also convince them to visit your website just to check out you interactive tools.
Lesson 5: Enhance their engagement and enjoyment while browsing.
One of the most important elements of creating a website is making sure that your visuals are eye-catching and entertaining to hook your audience, and encourage them to keep on scrolling on your website. By making your website more engaging you are ensuring that your audience is entertained, satisfied, and they are also more likely to come back and view your website once again. After this lesson you will learn the importance of keeping a close engagement with your viewers and audience.
Lesson objectives:
-Students will learn how website design and content can enhance user engagement and make browsing enjoyable.
For you, what makes a website fun or enjoyable to use? Those enjoyable features are designed by the developers to keep you from being bored while browsing through their website. Those elements ensure that your website keeps users interested, engaged, and satisfied while browsing, this in turn assures that they will have a great time while engaging with your website.
