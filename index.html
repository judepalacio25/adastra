Lesson 1: Efficiently design a website layout for easy navigation

Have you ever visited a website and you get immediately confused because you can’t find what you’re looking for due to its layout? May it be that you're a kid or an adult, designing your layout to be easy for navigation is ideal and useful for all users. Having a clear layout will help your users find the content they need from your website. In this lesson, you will learn how to efficiently design a website layout for easy navigation.


Lesson objectives:

- Identify key elements of a website layout.

- Explain why clear navigation improves user experience.

- Design a simple webpage layout that allows users to find information easily.


What makes a website easy or difficult to use? There are a lot of factors to consider which ensures the audience of your website comes around every now and then to check put your website. Everyone wants to have a good experience while visiting a website. No wants a website that is confusing, challenging or difficult to use. When creating a website always know that a better layout always equal to a better user friendly experience. A common example, if your website has a search feature, make sure to put your search bar at the top, upper left or upper right. Avoid putting your search bar somewhere hard to find or easily dismissable. Another example, your buttons like the home page or log in, should be where they can be easily spotted like at the top of your website or at the side.

Remember, a well-engineered website layout equals to a better experience for your users, may it be young users or old users. Below is a game called Navigation Flow, while playing it, observe where each part goes to. Make sure to put each part in its rightful place.

Lesson 2: Make sure that your textual content is clear, relevant and memorable. Also how to effectively communicate your website's intended message.

We can’t deny that the visuals of a website is important since it brings engagement and attracts users but we can’t also deny that the textual content of it is also important. It should be clearly written and does not confuse the readers, it should also be relevant depending on the website’s topic and most of all, it should be memorable to ensure that users revisit the website. It should also be taken into consideration with how the textual content is communicated. In this lesson you’ll learn how to make your textual content clear, relevant and memorable as well as how to effectively communicate it.


Lesson objectives:

- Students will understand how to create clear, relevant, and memorable textual content and learn how to effectively communicate a website's intended message.


On one hand, good website always have a clear goal or initiative, it would always have a proper message and direction. On the other hand, a bad website doesn't have a clear motive nor reason for existing. Your content must always be clear and your message must always be related to the theme of your website. If one thing about your website is confusing, or disconnected all of it will stop making sense, or it would just be borderline confusing. That is why you must have a clear message that is related to the intent of your website. For example, when choosing a font for your website, avoid using too stylish fonts as they may make your textual content confusing. Another example, never stray away from your core topic, if your website is about this then it should stay about that. Organizing your content in multiple paragraphs or categories will also help with avoiding confusing your users.

Remember, keeping content clear and organized will help attract users for your website since your content is one of the core selling points of your website. Below is a game called Clarity Sort where you must organize the text on the left to the green dots to the right.


Game Instructions

Match the text on the left to the green dots on the right.


Lesson 3: Provide clear and user-friendly functionality.

Back in the good old days, websites were known to be static, this means that the website wasn’t that much engaging and you can’t interact much with it. Nowadays, websites being interactive are highly needed, in line with this, it also needs to be user- friendly to avoid user confusion. In this lesson, you’ll learn how to provide a clear and user-friendly functionality for your own website.


Lesson objectives:

- Students will understand how to design websites with clear and user-friendly functionality so users can easily navigate and interact with the site.


Occam's Razor, where it is stated that the most simple solution is the best solution. Simplicity is key, to minimize confusion or eliminate it all together, don't be afraid to make your website layout as simple as possible. This won't make you website boring or lame, it will only ensure that your website is clear, easy to follow, and understandable. For example, don't be afraid to use more white space for your layout, remember the less cluttered it is the less stress it can give to your users. Another example, don't be afraid to also copy other user interfaces most importantly if their's is simpler and more organized, you can always use them as reference to better your website.

Remember, extravagance doesn't always equal to beauty, sometimes keeping it simple or light is enough. The game below called Fix The Flow, will show you how to build a website that is clear and user-friendly.
